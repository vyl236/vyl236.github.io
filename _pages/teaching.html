My teaching experience spans both journalism and strategic communication. I have taught five different courses as an instructor of record at the Missouri School of Journalism and served as a teaching assistant for nine additional courses at the University of Oklahoma. My teaching experience includes a wide range of instructional formats, from large lecture courses and theory-based classes to methodology, skills-based, and hands-on learning environments. I also engaged with curriculum development and have developed the first-ever course in Artificial Intelligence in Journalism at the University of Oklahoma.

My teaching philosophy focuses on progress rather than product. I strive to help students recognize their own growth by emphasizing continuous learning, reflection, and improvement rather than simply achieving a final outcome. I also incorporate inclusive teaching practices to create a classroom where all students feel welcomed, supported, and empowered to succeed. By fostering an engaging and collaborative learning environment, I encourage students to develop confidence, think critically, and apply their knowledge to real-world situations.

I am committed to continually growing as an educator. I hold a Graduate Certificate in College Teaching and have completed professional development in Inclusive Teaching, Online Teaching, and Teaching with AI. I regularly observe colleagues' classes, participate in mentoring conversations, and present my teaching strategies at national conferences. In recognition of my teaching innovation, I received the 2025 AEJMC Teaching News Terrifically in the 21st Century Award for developing a teaching approach that bridges science communication and journalism.
